Too many mods. Too much bloat. So we went back to vanilla Minecraft and added one thing: an MMO system built from scratch.

  • Group up & raid
  • Loot & leveling
  • No modpack needed
  • Java + Bedrock
  • Proximity voice chat

Seven classes, each with a role in the group. You carry one emblem on your hotbar: use it to swap into stance, where your abilities live, then swap back to build and mine. New abilities slot in as you level.
